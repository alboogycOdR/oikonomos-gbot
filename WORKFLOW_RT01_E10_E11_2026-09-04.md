**What's confirmed NOT wired (the real gaps, same shape as Gmail before Connectors-1):**
- Nothing calls `createRoutine`/`listRoutines` from `services/worker` or `apps/dashboard` — `ChatPage.tsx` passes `routines={[]}` hardcoded.
- No cron/timer mechanism actually fires a routine — `scheduler.ts` defines the ports, nothing implements them.
- `mailbox.ts`'s `send_to_role` is not registered as an invokable tool anywhere in `packages/broker`/`chatRunDriver` — a bot cannot actually call it today.
- **`pg-boss` is not a dependency anywhere in the repo.** OIK-105 is genuinely greenfield — everything above either doesn't need it yet or has ports waiting for it.
- **`org_facts` does not exist.** OIK-098–101 (the fact-publishing/ACL side of OME, as opposed to the handoff-messaging side) is genuinely greenfield.

This means: **E11's routine story and E10's handoff story are more "wire it up" than "build it from scratch."** Budget the wave accordingly — don't re-estimate from the WBS's raw S/M/L labels without adjusting for this.
